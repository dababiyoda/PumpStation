# Public Launch Ladder

Every promotion is a separate founder decision bound to an exact evidence packet and source revision. No agent may approve a promotion.

## Stage 0: Research sandbox

Synthetic and historical opportunities, paper portfolios, agent deliberation, reproducible tests, no external execution.

Exit requires threat model, adversarial tests, valid event chain, reproducible benchmark, preserved negative results, and zero unauthorized external effects.

## Stage 1: Closed private pilot

Invited participants, education, paper portfolios, consent, complaints, no pooled capital, no trading, no promotional campaigns.

Exit requires production identity, security review, participant protections, measured classifier errors, and incident response.

## Stage 2: Public research

Public education, transparent research, simulated opportunities, audited methodology. This is not a fund, token sale, investment offer, or coordinated-buy community.

Exit requires legal review, multilingual disclosures, moderation, independent methodology review, and separation of research from promotion.

## Stage 3: Founder-owned proprietary capital

Requires explicit founder authorization, qualified legal review, separate books, company/founder-owned capital only, allowlists, loss limits, independent reconciliation, and no position-linked public promotion.

## Stage 4: Productive-asset operations

Businesses, infrastructure, software, validators, equipment, or other revenue-producing assets. Requires Kernel integration, legal operator, receipts, verified outcomes, and closed obligations.

## Stage 5: Regulated external capital

Outside capital requires counsel, legal vehicle, eligibility rules, offering documents, custody, valuation, administration, audit, conflicts policy, fiduciary accountability, and explicit founder action. Software cannot authorize this stage.
