# Evidence-Gated Public Launch Ladder

No stage advances because the product feels safe. Every transition requires a version-bound packet with passed evidence, failed evidence, unresolved risks, legal and security status, founder decision, rollback, kill criteria, and a cryptographic hash.

Founder approval is necessary but not sufficient. PumpStation records an approved request as `awaiting_kernel_permit`; the active stage remains unchanged because this repository has no Kernel permit interface.

## Stage 0: Research sandbox

Capabilities:

- synthetic opportunities and clearly labeled fixtures;
- historical replay and simulated portfolios;
- agent deliberation and structured community review;
- no external execution.

Advancement evidence:

- complete threat model;
- anti-manipulation tests pass;
- deterministic, valid event chain;
- zero unauthorized external effects;
- reproducible simulations;
- negative, zero, and harmful results preserved.

## Stage 1: Closed private pilot

Capabilities:

- invited users;
- education and paper portfolios;
- community research;
- no pooled capital, execution, or promotional campaigns.

Advancement evidence:

- identity and access controls;
- informed participant consent;
- conflict disclosure;
- complaints, appeals, and correction;
- measured false-positive and false-negative rates;
- independent security review;
- tested incident response.

## Stage 2: Public research platform

Public means education, transparent research, simulated opportunities, and audited methodology.

It does not mean a securities offering, fund, token sale, coordinated buying program, custody, or acceptance of user funds.

Advancement evidence:

- qualified legal review;
- standardized disclosures;
- multilingual risk information;
- moderation and anti-affinity-fraud controls;
- independently reviewed methodology;
- operational separation of research from promotion.

## Stage 3: Founder-owned proprietary capital pilot

This stage may begin only after a new explicit founder decision, qualified legal review, and Kernel consequence permit.

Requirements:

- company-owned or founder-owned capital only;
- separate legal and accounting records;
- asset and counterparty allowlists;
- capital, position, transaction, and loss ceilings;
- no outside investors;
- no public promotion tied to positions;
- independent reconciliation, emergency revocation, and complete receipts.

## Stage 4: Productive-asset operations

Priority assets:

- immigrant-owned operating businesses;
- software and revenue-producing digital services;
- validators and productive infrastructure;
- small-business acquisitions;
- legally structured real estate or equipment vehicles;
- milestone-based venture financing.

Returns must derive from operating cash flow, fees, productivity, customer value, productive infrastructure, or fundamental long-term appreciation.

## Stage 5: Regulated external-capital vehicle

This stage is not automatically implementable.

It requires qualified counsel, a selected legal structure, regulatory classification, investor eligibility, offering documents, custody, valuation policy, independent administration, audit, conflicts policy, fiduciary accountability, insurance or reserves, and a new explicit founder decision.

The SEC notes that persons effecting securities transactions for others, finding investors, receiving transaction-related compensation, handling funds, or operating trading platforms may trigger broker-dealer questions: [SEC Guide to Broker-Dealer Registration](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-trading-markets/division-trading-markets-compliance-guides/guide-broker-dealer-registration). PumpStation makes no legal classification and implements none of these activities.

## Promotion packet invariant

Every version contains:

- current and requested stage;
- exact source version;
- passed and failed evidence;
- unresolved risks;
- legal and security review status;
- founder decision;
- Kernel permit status;
- timestamp;
- rollback plan;
- kill criteria;
- packet hash.

No agent may approve a promotion. No local method activates a stage.
