# ADR-0001: Replace Market Coordination with a Simulation-First Community Capital Institution

- Status: Accepted for experiment
- Decision owner: Founder
- Deliberation: `DELIBERATION-0001.json`

## Context

The repository described coordinated memecoin buying and automated exits. The clarified founder intention is an immigrant-led, agent-operated institution that creates productive ownership while remaining lawful, evidence-bound, zero-trust, and subordinate to UNIIMENTE governance.

## Decision

Replace the active doctrine and executable surface with a simulation-only opportunity, deliberation, authorization, evidence, and reconciliation runtime. Preserve wallet identity only as proof of address control. Prohibit market manipulation and all real external effects.

## Consequences

The repository aligns with current intent and creates a testable institutional skeleton. It is not revenue-producing, production-authenticated, legally structured, or evidence that multi-agent complexity outperforms simpler workflows.

## Reversal

Close the draft PR or revert its commits. Main remains unchanged until an authorized merge.
