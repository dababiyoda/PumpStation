# PumpStation Product Constitution

Status: proposed domain constitution, subordinate to UNIIMENTE Kernel.

## Purpose

Increase lawful productive ownership, institutional research capacity, fraud resistance, and long-term economic capability for immigrants and underserved communities.

## Governing rule

Models reason. Agents and members propose. Authorized humans decide. Only the UNIIMENTE Kernel may permit production external consequences. Reality determines what worked.

## Hard invariants

- `UNAUTHORIZED_EXTERNAL_EFFECTS = 0`.
- No actor may expand its own permissions.
- Wallet ownership does not create institutional authority.
- Model output is not evidence by itself.
- Dissent, conflicts, negative evidence, and failed outcomes remain visible.
- Silence is never approval.
- No public or capital stage advances automatically.

## Prohibited conduct

No coordinated price movement, wash trading, fake volume, spoofing, promotion after accumulation, concealed holdings or compensation, guaranteed returns, fabricated partnerships, fake accounts, deceptive scarcity, coordinated exit liquidity, loyalty pressure, evidence suppression, self-approval, or logging bypass.

## Participant rights

Plain-language and multilingual disclosure, informed consent, cooling-off, conflict visibility, protected dissent, complaint and appeal paths, withdrawal rules, no retaliation, and no reputation penalty for refusing an opportunity.

## Closure

Activity, code, messages, workflow completion, model confidence, or payment alone do not prove success. Consequential closure requires authorized action, accepted delivery, independently checkable outcome, resolved obligations, reconciled economics, and no unresolved critical harm or authority failure.
