# PumpStation Roadmap

## Phase 0: Constitutional conversion

Replace unsafe doctrine, preserve history, install founder-intent and collaboration records, enforce simulation-only operation.

## Phase 1: Research and deliberation laboratory

Typed opportunities, source provenance, bull and bear cases, conflicts, security, legal questions, community impact, reproducible simulations, and benchmark against simpler workflows.

## Phase 2: Semantic immune boundary

Source trust classification, untrusted-data isolation, semantic injection analysis, quarantine, contradiction preservation, and measured false-positive/false-negative rates.

## Phase 3: UNIIMENTE Kernel integration

Canonical identity, legal principals, policy decisions, capability grants, budgets, commit witnesses, Consequence Gate, receipts, and causal events. Remove header-based authority scaffolding.

## Phase 4: Closed community pilot

Education and paper portfolios with consent, multilingual disclosures, protected dissent, complaints, moderation, and no capital custody.

## Phase 5: Productive-asset laboratory

Underwrite small businesses, infrastructure, software, and productive crypto operations. Simulate acquisition, improvement, cash flow, downside, and governance.

## Phase 6: Founder-owned capital canary

Only after legal and security review. Narrow allowlists, ceilings, expiry, revocation, independent custody/reconciliation, and no public promotion tied to positions.

## Phase 7: Operating Venture Cells

Acquire, build, or improve productive assets through Kernel-governed cells. Seal reusable capabilities only after accepted delivery and verified outcomes.

## Phase 8: Selective shared-trust rail

Use credentials, multisig, escrow, proof anchors, and settlement records only where multiple parties need shared verification.

## Phase 9: External-capital readiness

Reserved. No claim of completion without qualified counsel, custody, administration, audit, offering documents, fiduciary accountability, and founder authorization.
