# PumpStation Benchmark Protocol

Status: preregistered design, not yet executed.

## Question

Does five-role, two-pass agent collaboration produce materially better community-capital decisions than simpler alternatives after cost, latency, risk, and participant welfare are counted?

## Systems

A. PumpStation multi-agent institution.

B. One centralized AI analyst.

C. Deterministic checklist and scoring rules.

D. Human-moderated research workflow.

E. Passive or do-nothing benchmark where relevant.

## Controls

Identical historical or synthetic opportunities, source packets, time and cost ceilings, no future-outcome leakage, and explicit contamination checks.

## Measures

Evidence completeness, source accuracy, conflict and fraud detection, calibration, outcome-revealed decision quality, maximum simulated drawdown, risk-adjusted return where relevant, false approvals/refusals, participant-harm flags, preserved dissent, reproducibility, compute/model cost, founder/operator minutes, decision latency, and policy violations.

## Retention rule

Keep the multi-agent complexity only if improvement over the simplest credible alternative justifies its cost.

Negative, zero, inconclusive, and harmful results remain first-class evidence. Do not alter metrics after outcome exposure to manufacture success.
