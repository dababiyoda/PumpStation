# Recursive Founder-Intent Collaboration Protocol

Use for identity, money, external effects, participant rights, shared contracts, public claims, capital stages, or cross-repository architecture.

1. State inspected scope, source revisions, and unknowns.
2. Classify the change as lightweight, standard, or constitutional.
3. Update the Founder Intent Ledger.
4. State canonical and prohibited ownership.
5. Compare baseline, do nothing, simplest alternative, strongest competitor, and reversible experiment.
6. Record distinct analysis from Founder-Intent Steward, Systems Architect, Adversarial Reviewer, Operator and Maintainer, and Evidence and Welfare Guardian.
7. Pass 1: amplify advantages, assign stable IDs to every disadvantage, redesign or reject.
8. Pass 2: attack the strengthened design, expose new bureaucracy and fragility, disposition every Pass-1 disadvantage, and preserve residual risks.
9. Choose `RETAIN`, `REGRESS`, `KILL`, `DEFER`, `EXPERIMENT`, or `NEEDS_FOUNDER_DECISION`.
10. Implement on a dedicated branch and draft PR.
11. Report evidence tier, tests, rollback, kill criteria, unchanged items, dissent, and next decisive test.

No intention, downside, dissent, negative result, sealed history, or obligation may disappear silently. No PR may claim capital operation or commercial success from code, fixtures, or simulation.
