# PumpStation Institutional Charter

## Status

PumpStation Labs is a Stage 0 research sandbox and a bounded Venture Cell inside the wider UNIIMENTE architecture.

**SIMULATION ONLY. NO REAL MONEY. NO INVESTMENT OFFER. NO COORDINATED MARKET ACTION. NO PUBLIC ASSET PROMOTION. MODEL OUTPUT IS NOT FINANCIAL EVIDENCE.**

## Purpose

PumpStation helps immigrants and underserved communities combine knowledge, skills, relationships, and eventually governed capital to identify, acquire, build, improve, and retain productive assets.

The institution exists to increase:

- investment education and shared research capacity;
- transparent opportunity analysis;
- fraud and affinity-exploitation protection;
- access to productive ownership;
- business-building and acquisition intelligence;
- portable reputation and institutional memory;
- long-term community economic capability.

Immigrant identity creates multilingual intelligence, trusted research collaboration, overlooked-market discovery, business relationships, and collective operating capacity. It never substitutes for evidence or creates a duty to trust, invest, recruit, or remain silent.

## Permanent authority rule

> Models reason. Agents propose. Policies and authorized humans decide. The UNIIMENTE Kernel determines what may become real. Reality determines what was correct.

PumpStation may discover, research, compare, simulate, compile packets, preserve dissent, recommend, monitor approved simulations, and reconcile paper outcomes.

PumpStation may not create its own authority, move money, place trades, accept investor funds, contact counterparties, publish owned-asset promotions, activate a strategy, change constitutional controls, or bypass the UNIIMENTE Consequence Gate.

Agent consensus has no authority multiplier. Repetition does not improve evidence quality.

## Institutional sequence

```text
community opportunity
→ independent research
→ conflict disclosure
→ adversarial underwriting
→ legal and risk classification
→ transparent review
→ authorized simulation
→ governed human decision
→ Kernel permit, if ever implemented
→ productive operation
→ independently verified result
→ transparent gains and losses
→ retained institutional learning
```

Stage 0 stops before any Kernel permit, productive operation, capital movement, or external consequence.

## Participant rights

Every participant has the right to:

- plain-language and eventually multilingual risk information;
- decline any opportunity without reputation penalty;
- see organizer holdings and compensation;
- preserve and submit dissent;
- complain, appeal, correct, and withdraw where applicable;
- receive no guarantee of outcomes;
- face no coercive recruitment or retaliation;
- distinguish claims, models, fixtures, simulations, paper results, transactions, verified outcomes, and commercial results.

## Evidence doctrine

Model output can propose a claim or research path. It is not financial evidence.

Conversation volume, reactions, enthusiasm, popularity, nationality, family status, religion, and community loyalty are not evidence.

Cryptographic integrity can show who signed or whether data changed. It does not make the underlying claim true. The W3C states that verifiability of a credential does not imply truth of the claims encoded in it: [Verifiable Credentials Data Model v2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/#terminology).

## Single Bottleneck Metric

The initial Single Bottleneck Metric is:

> Percentage of simulated opportunity decisions with complete evidence, preserved dissent, reproducible results, and zero prohibited coordination.

Gross simulated return never outranks truth, risk, participant protection, or legal authority.

## Amendment

This charter cannot be changed by an agent, model output, popularity vote, or local code path. A proposed amendment requires preserved lineage, a constitutional deliberation, explicit authorized-human decision, and compatibility with the canonical UNIIMENTE Kernel. PumpStation itself contains no mechanism to grant that compatibility.
