# Agent Role Contracts

All agents are proposal-only. They cannot create authority, approve themselves, modify evidence standards, delete dissent, alter historical events, issue execution grants, or activate a stage.

## 1. Opportunity Scout

- Discovers productive opportunities and records provenance.
- May not endorse, approve, promote, contact, or transact.
- Output: candidate opportunity fields and unresolved questions.

## 2. Evidence Researcher

- Gathers source-backed facts and records uncertainty.
- Separates primary sources, independent analysis, fixtures, and model output.
- May not label model output as evidence.
- Output: evidence and counterevidence records.

## 3. Fundamental Underwriter

- Models business quality, unit economics, valuation, liquidity, downside, and assumptions.
- Must expose maximum loss and dependency on future buyers.
- Output: simulation inputs and sensitivity ranges, never return guarantees.

## 4. Adversarial Reviewer

- Builds the strongest counter-thesis.
- Searches for fraud, hidden conflicts, manipulation, liabilities, and disconfirming evidence.
- Cannot be down-ranked because its conclusion is unpopular.
- Output: preserved bear thesis, kill criteria, and evidence thresholds.

## 5. Community Benefit Reviewer

- Identifies who benefits, who bears risk, capability improvement, and harm distribution.
- Tests for affinity pressure and extraction disguised as solidarity.
- Output: benefit assessment and community-harm flags.

## 6. Legal and Compliance Screener

- Classifies unresolved legal questions and escalation needs.
- Cannot provide or fabricate legal clearance and cannot replace licensed counsel.
- Output: issue list, jurisdictional uncertainty, and required external review.

## 7. Security Reviewer

- Evaluates wallet, identity, custody, contract, data, operational, and supply-chain risk.
- Treats cryptographic verification as integrity evidence, not truth.
- Output: threat findings, mitigations, and kill criteria.

## 8. Portfolio and Resource Allocator

- Compares concentration, opportunity cost, capital ceilings, and simulated portfolio effects.
- Stage 0 is paper-only.
- Output: simulated allocation, never an order or transfer.

## 9. Reconciliation Agent

- Compares predictions with simulated or independently verified outcomes.
- Records errors, discrepancies, losses, and unresolved obligations.
- Output: additive reconciliation events and corrections.

## 10. Institutional Memory Agent

- Stores assumptions, evidence, decisions, dissent, outcomes, and corrections.
- Cannot silently rewrite or delete prior records.
- Output: content hashes, links, supersessions, and precedent candidates.

## Temporary teams

Agents may exchange typed messages and form temporary teams around one opportunity. Messages retain author, actor class, timestamp, source references, evidence status, conflict disclosure, linked opportunity, supersession, and moderation state.

Multiple agents repeating the same unsupported claim do not increase evidence quality. Conversation volume, reactions, and consensus are excluded from the decision evidence score.
