## Mandatory founder rule

> **Preserve the intended effect. Do not literalize the metaphor. Search reality before inventing architecture.**

Do not force blockchain, DAO, DeFi, tokens, zero trust, morphogenesis, swarms, organs, or any other favored vocabulary into a design because Alfonso used an analogy. Extract the useful mechanism and keep it only when it improves the named effect.

For every proposed mechanism ask:

- what real outcome is required?
- can existing PumpStation/UNIIMENTE capability solve it?
- can mature open source, commodity software, an API/service/plugin, or computer use solve it?
- what causal mechanism from Web3 or another domain is actually valuable?
- what residual CapabilityDeficit remains?
- what evidence shows the proposed mechanism beats the simpler alternative?

Canonical morphogenesis means functional capability formation/reconfiguration in response to verified need. It does not require digital biological structures or dynamic organizations.

## Web3 interpretation

Use Web3 mechanisms only for precise jobs such as attribution, shared ownership, programmable commitments, settlement, bounded governance, portable credentials, or verifiable public state. Do not put a process on-chain merely because PumpStation is Web3-native.

## Authority and evidence

PumpStation may become highly capable; it does not become constitutionally sovereign. New agents, smart contracts, tools, wallets, code, or capabilities never self-authorize or inherit founder authority.

Building != installation != activation != spending/trading/contact/consequence authority.

Judge success by truthful participant/economic outcomes and verified system behavior—not by how futuristic, decentralized, organism-like, or crypto-native the architecture appears.
