# PumpStation Recursive Collaboration Protocol

Every material change must preserve founder intent, counterevidence, dissent, authority limits, rollback, and exact validation.

## Required roles

1. Founder-Intent Steward
2. Systems Architect
3. Adversarial Reviewer
4. Operator and Maintainer
5. Evidence and Welfare Guardian

Do not collapse distinct positions into manufactured consensus.

## Exactly two strengthening passes

Pass 1 states the intended outcome, strongest advantages, disadvantages with IDs, redesigns, baseline, do-nothing option, simplest viable alternative, strongest competing architecture, reversible experiment, and revival evidence.

Pass 2 attacks the strengthened design for bureaucracy, centralization, fragility, overfit, expense, incomprehensibility, irreversibility, gaming, and dependency. Every Pass 1 disadvantage receives an explicit disposition. The result must be one of:

- `RETAIN`
- `REGRESS`
- `KILL`
- `DEFER`
- `EXPERIMENT`
- `NEEDS_FOUNDER_DECISION`

Do not add a hidden third pass. New material evidence opens a new linked decision.

## Evidence ladder

Do not equate:

1. prose claim;
2. model output;
3. deterministic fixture;
4. unit test;
5. sandbox execution;
6. real external effect;
7. independently verified outcome;
8. commercial result.

## Authority

Models and agents can recommend only. Constitutional or authority-expanding action requires an authorized human and, for external consequences, the canonical UNIIMENTE Kernel. PumpStation cannot approve its own expansion.
